# ADR-128: Keep reasoning checks, remove presentation rituals

> 2026-09-16 · Accepted.
> Revises the mandatory presentation and closing-offer rules in ADR-007/015/028/077.
> No skill is added or retired; the marketplace remains four plugins and 23 skills.

## Decision

The owner requested three changes together: remove compulsory next-action menus,
scale reasoning outputs to the question instead of filling a complete worksheet,
and stop requiring metaphors or banning technical words.

Frame's value is the reasoning it checks, not a printed shape. First-principles
still distinguishes grounded premises from convention and reports divergence;
orthogonal still tests independence; dialectic still strengthens both sides,
checks the inference, separates contrary evidence from missing evidence, and
identifies the deciding test. A short answer must retain the material finding,
evidence, and uncertainty, but need not print every intermediate heading.

Analogize still provides a structurally checked analogy when that is the requested
task, with its relevant limitations. Elsewhere an analogy is an optional aid.
Technical vocabulary is allowed when useful, with a brief explanation when the
reader needs it. Existing worked examples illustrate the method, not an output form.

## Continuation and authority

Frame and shape no longer require a closing chooser or repeated opt-out. A
completed analysis-only or mockup-only task may simply end. A useful suggestion
is optional. When the larger request already authorizes execution and the
decision is settled, return to that workflow and its checks without asking again.
The finding or selected mockup is not itself authority to build, change durable
priorities, or run a paid experiment. Ask the specific unresolved question when
intent or authority is genuinely missing.

Dogfood's run scope remains checked before driving the real interface. Existing
scoped approval counts; unapproved external, destructive, or paid effects remain
blocked. Missing authority is not disguised as a mandatory next-step menu, and
unrun intents must be reported. A paid full-cost pass is not an automatic finale.

## Source and adapters must agree

The old Codex and Cursor adapters injected a forced closing-menu contract into
five skills even if the prose was softened. Remove that injection from the four
ordinary analysis/convergence consumers. Dogfood retains a conditional pre-run
authority contract, not an end-of-session routing gate. Canaries check that the
old menu is absent while missing-authority protections remain.

Bodies, conditional references, anti-pattern tables, plugin conventions, public
surfaces, and generated installations are updated together. Historical ADRs and
observations remain evidence of earlier choices, not current instructions.

## Boundaries and verification

This does not remove mockup's interactive-HTML deliverable, probe's experimental
validity checks, handoff compatibility, explicit invocation, user-owned decisions,
or evidence requirements. Global personal style settings are outside this change.

Verification covers generation/installation consistency, regression canaries,
negative fixtures, and inspection of the retained reasoning and authority checks.
It does not establish that the revised skills outperform their predecessors in
real conversations; that needs subsequent use, not wording-matching tests.
